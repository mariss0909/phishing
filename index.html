    How to use the phishing detector

    Follow these quick steps to review a suspicious message, verify the sender, and decide whether it is safe or dangerous.

    QUICK USER GUIDE

    Step-by-step

    1. 1. Paste the messageCopy the full text exactly as received. The detector checks urgency, login requests, password mentions, payment lures, and suspicious domains.
    2. 2. Enter the senderType the sender email or phone number in the sender field. The system compares it with your saved trusted contacts and official channels.
    3. 3. Review the sender verdict firstThe app prioritizes the sender and official family domain. If the sender matches the real organization, it stays safe or green even if the message wording is extra urgent.
    4. 4. Check the linksUse the preview option only when needed. It shows readable content from the URL without opening the full page in a browser.
    5. 5. Verify through official contact channelsOpen official apps, websites, or phone numbers you already know. Do not trust links or contact details inside the suspicious message.
    6. 6. Save trusted contactsStore bank, business, and support contacts with their official source URL so future checks are faster and more accurate.

    Features and functions

    1. Sender verificationChecks whether the sender or domain matches trusted contacts, saved contacts, or an official company family domain.
    2. Message analysisFlags pressure tactics, login prompts, password requests, payment pressure, and other suspicious message patterns.
    3. Link previewShows the linked page text and checks the host without exposing the user to a full interactive browsing session.
    4. Threat reportsStores analyzed reports locally with risk level, sender, findings, and host information so users can review past checks.
    5. Official contact directoryLists verified support channels from banks, government services, apps, utilities, and media organizations.
    6. Saved trusted contactsLets the user keep multiple emails and phone numbers for the same organization and edit or remove them anytime.
